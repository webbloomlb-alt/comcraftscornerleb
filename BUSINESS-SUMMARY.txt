CRAFTS CORNER — BUSINESS SUMMARY

Crafts Corner is an online crafts and DIY store in Lebanon. It sells creative project kits, yarn, and tools for hobbies, handmade gifts, and personal craft projects.

PRODUCT RANGE
- Crochet and amigurumi kits, including small characters and flower bouquets
- Diamond painting kits, including scenic and religious designs
- Punch needle kits for hoops, tote bags, and pillows
- Embroidery and cross stitch kits
- Latch hook kits
- Yarn and yarn packs: cotton, acrylic, macrame, multicolor, micropolyester, wool, and T-shirt yarn
- Craft tools and accessories, including hooks, needles, hoops, embroidery floss, stitch markers, and amigurumi safety eyes
- Creative gift boxes and gift vouchers

CUSTOMERS
People looking for a creative hobby, materials for their next project, or a craft-related gift. The product range includes options for beginners as well as experienced makers.
