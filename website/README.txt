BUSINESS DESCRIPTION
Crafts Corner is an online crafts and DIY store in Lebanon, selling project kits, yarn, and tools for creative hobbies and handmade gifts. The range includes crochet and amigurumi, diamond painting, punch needle, embroidery, cross stitch, latch hook, yarn, hooks, needles, and accessories. Gift boxes and vouchers are also listed on the existing store.

SCOPE
A responsive showcase and curated 12-product catalogue with category filters, search, sorting, product/design details, and saved inquiry picks. WhatsApp opens a prepared inquiry for the visitor to review and send. No messages are sent automatically, no payments are collected, and no live inventory is connected. Design availability and final prices must be confirmed with the store.
